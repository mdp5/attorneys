About

A Chicago-based practice focused entirely on real estate — because property law rewards depth, not breadth.

ZB

[Bio placeholder — replace with your background.] Zoe G. Biel represents property owners throughout Chicago and Cook County in residential and commercial real estate matters, with particular attention to the disputes that arise after closing: condominium association conflicts, clouded title, and property tax exposure.

Clients come to this practice at very different moments — some mid-purchase, some mid-dispute with a board, some untangling a title problem left by a previous owner. The approach stays the same: read the governing documents and the record closely, explain the real options in plain terms, and move deliberately toward resolution.

Practice Areas

Everything related to real estate, under one roof — so the same attorney who reviews your contract can also defend your title.

§ Closings

Residential & Commercial Closings

Contract review, negotiation, and closing representation for buyers and sellers across the Chicago area.

§ Associations

Condominium & HOA Disputes

Representing individual unit owners in conflicts with their association — assessments, governance, maintenance, and board conduct.

§ Title

Title Issues & Quiet Title

Clearing clouded title, resolving old liens and undischarged mortgages, and quiet title actions where the record needs correcting.

§ Leasing

Landlord–Tenant Matters

Lease disputes and Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance compliance for owners and small landlords.

§ Tax

Property Tax Appeals & Exemptions

Assessment appeals and exemption issues before the Cook County Assessor, including Senior Freeze and Homeowner Exemption matters.

§ Zoning

Zoning & Land Use

Variances, municipal code compliance, and permitting issues for Chicago property owners and developers.
